PeterPlate

A campus dining ecosystem, designed from zero by someone who had been building it.

Role

UI/UX Designer
Software developer

Team

Information and Computer Science Student Council

Timeframe

October 2026 - Present

Focus

  • Journey mapping
  • User interviews
  • Usability testing
  • Figma handoff

Overview

PeterPlate is a dining ecosystem for UC Irvine students: menus, hall information and the daily decision of where to eat, in one place across web and mobile. I joined the team as a software developer in October 2025, building frontend and backend features. In January 2026 I moved into the designer seat on the same product, which meant I already knew what every proposed screen would cost to build.

The problem

Campus dining information was scattered across pages that were never written for the decision a hungry student is actually making. The question is rarely “what is the menu” and almost always “where should I eat right now, given the time I have and what I can eat.”

Research

I ran user interviews with students across different dining patterns and mapped the journey from the moment the decision starts to the moment they sit down. Journey mapping is what turned a content problem into a decision problem, and it changed what the home screen needed to answer first.

Designing it

I built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma with accessibility considered from the first pass rather than audited at the end, then put them in front of students in usability testing and revised what did not hold up.

Working with developers

In Agile and Scrum ceremonies I produced design specs, prioritised deliverables, and kept handoff technically feasible, which is easier when you have written the components yourself.

Consistency across touchpoints

Web and mobile share one system, so a student moving between them is never relearning the interface.

Where it stands

The product is in active development with the student council team. This case study will expand with final screens, research artefacts and outcomes as the work ships.
