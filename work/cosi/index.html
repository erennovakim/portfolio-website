Cosi

Making household contribution visible, so housemates argue less about it.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Team

Design at UCI

Timeframe

January 2026 - March 2026

Focus

  • User research
  • Personas
  • Design systems
  • Prototyping

Overview

Cosi is an app for the friction of living together. It combines task management with communication flows designed to stay conflict-neutral, and it makes each person's contribution visible so the conversation is about facts rather than impressions.

The research

I ran user research with people living in shared housing and built personas and user flows from what they described. The recurring pattern was not laziness. It was invisibility: people underestimate what housemates do when they do not see it happen, and the resulting conversation starts as an accusation.

Most chore arguments are not about chores. They are about not being seen.

The design response

Conflict-neutral flows

The app phrases requests as shared state rather than personal complaint, so raising something does not require confrontation.

Visible contribution

Contribution is visualised and incentivised, which replaces the argument's evidence problem with a shared picture.

System work

I built the branding and design system in Figma first, so the high-fidelity prototypes stayed consistent as the flows grew, and tested the proposed solutions with users rather than shipping assumptions.
