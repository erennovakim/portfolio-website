Ēkyu

Selective noise cancellation, designed with the person it was built for.

Focus

Overview

Ēkyu is an integrated hardware and software solution for selective noise cancellation: instead of removing all sound or none of it, the wearer decides what stays. I designed the mobile controller app, the physical gesture system, the icon set and the brand identity, and wrote the full case study inside a 40-hour sprint.

Designing with, not for

The feature set came out of interviewing a hearing-impaired individual and confirming each design choice with them rather than assuming what would help. That conversation is why the product sorts sound by what the wearer wants to keep, rather than treating all noise as a problem to remove.

Accessibility decisions belong to the people they affect.

The principle the sprint kept returning to

Two surfaces, one system

The controller app

Custom-built icons carry the sound vocabulary, so a source is recognisable before the label is read.

The gesture system

Physical gestures handle the adjustments people make mid-conversation, when reaching for a phone is the wrong answer.
