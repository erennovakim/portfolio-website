## Overview

Ēkyu is an integrated hardware and software solution for selective noise cancellation: instead of
removing all sound or none of it, the wearer decides what stays. I designed the mobile controller
app, the physical gesture system, the icon set and the brand identity, and wrote the full case
study inside a 40-hour sprint.

## Designing with, not for

The feature set came out of interviewing a hearing-impaired individual and confirming each design
choice with them rather than assuming what would help. That conversation is why the product sorts
sound by what the wearer wants to keep, rather than treating all noise as a problem to remove.

<Pullquote from="The principle the sprint kept returning to">
  Accessibility decisions belong to the people they affect.
</Pullquote>
