## Overview

PeterPlate is a dining ecosystem for UC Irvine students: menus, hall information and the daily
decision of where to eat, in one place across web and mobile. I joined the team as a software
developer in October 2025, building frontend and backend features. In January 2026 I moved into
the designer seat on the same product, which meant I already knew what every proposed screen
would cost to build.

## The problem

Campus dining information was scattered across pages that were never written for the decision a
hungry student is actually making. The question is rarely "what is the menu" and almost always
"where should I eat right now, given the time I have and what I can eat."

## Research

I ran user interviews with students across different dining patterns and mapped the journey from
the moment the decision starts to the moment they sit down. Journey mapping is what turned a
content problem into a decision problem, and it changed what the home screen needed to answer
first.

<Pullquote>
  Designing the product I had been building taught me which constraints were real and which were
  just habit.
</Pullquote>

## Designing it

I built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma with accessibility considered from the first pass
rather than audited at the end, then put them in front of students in usability testing and
revised what did not hold up.
