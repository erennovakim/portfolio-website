## Overview

Cosi is an app for the friction of living together. It combines task management with
communication flows designed to stay conflict-neutral, and it makes each person's contribution
visible so the conversation is about facts rather than impressions.

## The research

I ran user research with people living in shared housing and built personas and user flows from
what they described. The recurring pattern was not laziness. It was invisibility: people
underestimate what housemates do when they do not see it happen, and the resulting conversation
starts as an accusation.

## System work

I built the branding and design system in Figma first, so the high-fidelity prototypes stayed
consistent as the flows grew, and tested the proposed solutions with users rather than shipping
assumptions.
